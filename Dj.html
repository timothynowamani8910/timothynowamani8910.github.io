Our DJs

Meet some of the people behind the music industry in Uganda.

Featured DJ  Etania Mutoni

Etania Mutoni is the heartbeat of Uganda's nightlife. As the "Life of the Party," she brings unmatched energy, passion, and unforgettable performances to every stage she touches.

From hosting NTV's "Mix Party" to headlining sold-out concerts across East Africa, Etania continues to redefine what it means to be a DJ in the modern era.
