Who Is Etania?

Etania Mutoni is a Ugandan DJ, TV host, and social media influencer who has become one of East Africa's most prominent female entertainers. Known as the "Life of the Party," she has redefined what it means to be a DJ in Uganda.

🎤 The Rise to Fame

Etania first rose to national prominence as the host of NTV's "Mix Party" during the COVID-19 lockdown. Her high-energy persona and music mixes brought the party into people's living rooms, quickly making her a household name.

🎵 The "Life of the Party" Show

On April 4, 2026, Etania headlined her debut concert, "Becoming The Life of the Party," at the Hockey Grounds in Kampala. The event was a massive success, drawing a packed crowd and featuring an electrifying performance with 34 dancers.

💪 A Role Model

Beyond the music, Etania is a role model for aspiring female DJs and entertainers. She lost her father at age 10 and became a provider for her mother and two younger sisters, a responsibility that continues to drive her success.
