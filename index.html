About Etania Mutoni

Etania Mutoni is Uganda's premier female DJ, TV host, and social media influencer. Known as the "Life of the Party," she rose to fame as the host of NTV's "Mix Party" during the COVID-19 lockdown.

From a simple Masaka girl to headlining sold-out concerts at Kampala's Hockey Grounds, Etania has become one of East Africa's most influential entertainers.
